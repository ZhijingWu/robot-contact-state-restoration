# Limitations and permitted claims

Supported: independent contact simulation; official components reused; sixteen paired seeds; physical deviations from omissions; 0/16 selected-command changes; exact measured complete-state controls; historical independent-process equality of 448 NPZ files / 1,424 numeric arrays; incremental negative decision-impact finding.

Not supported: a native converted Agentic Real2Sim episode reproduction; a physical robot test; a learned-policy result; a framework defect; a new restoration algorithm; general decision equivalence; real-world force accuracy; universal or cross-platform bitwise determinism; supervision, collaboration or endorsement by Chenfanfu Jiang, UCLA or upstream authors.

The recorded compiled-model SHA-256 is retained as provenance only. Its MJB payload is excluded because it embeds external robot geometry; users rebuild the model from independently acquired assets. Absolute paths/container metadata may alter serialized model/file hashes even when the measured dynamics agree. Full restoration covers the declared MuJoCo integration vector with equal model/configuration and controller tape, plus wrapper frame. The diagnostic Python time counter is reconstructed. Arbitrary Python/external/controller state is not claimed serialized.

The complete native converted-episode test remains unperformed. The independent scene supplies neither reconstructed DROID object correspondence nor native visual/calibration/physical-prior evidence. The contact finding does not update that status.

The large candidate margin is an observed feature of this setup, not evidence that a tuned adversarial margin or another contact geometry would behave the same. Contact onset belongs to the shared prefix; post-checkpoint evolution was tested. Future work should identify contact/controller/margin regimes before claiming downstream relevance.
